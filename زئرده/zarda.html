🐦 Zarda, the Little Bird

Page 1

The Playful Little Bird

In a beautiful forest, a little bird named Zarda lived with her mother in a warm nest high up in a big tree.

Zarda loved flying, playing, and exploring every corner of the forest.

But her mother always told her: "Zarda, don't fly too far from the nest, so you don't get lost."

Zarda would reply, "Okay, Mommy!" But deep down, she didn't really listen to her mother's advice.

Page 2

Where Did Zarda Go?

One day, while Zarda was playing near the nest, she saw a tiny worm crawling through the grass. She said happily, "I'll fly right after it!"

She kept following the worm from place to place until she drifted far away from her nest without noticing.

Suddenly, Zarda stopped and looked around. Her nest was nowhere to be found! She panicked, "Oh my! Where am I? I've lost my way home!"

Page 3

The Kind Rabbit

Zarda started walking between the trees, crying quietly. After a short while, she met a friendly rabbit.

The rabbit asked, "Why are you crying, little bird?"

Zarda said, "I'm lost and I don't know how to get back home."

The rabbit thought for a moment and asked, "Do you remember anything near your nest?"

Zarda replied, "Yes! There was a yellow flag right beside our nest."

The rabbit smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will help you find your way home."

Page 4

The Hungry Bird

The rabbit and Zarda walked together through the forest searching for the way. After a long walk, Zarda said, "I'm so hungry!"

The rabbit replied, "I know a little farm by the river that has delicious corn. Come on, let's grab a bite and then continue our journey."

They went to the farm and started eating the corn. But suddenly... they heard a loud noise! "Woof! Get away from the farm!"

The guard dog had spotted them and started running right after them.

Page 5

Into the River!

Terrified, the rabbit and Zarda ran as fast as they could. But without realizing it... they reached the edge of the river! Suddenly, they slipped and fell into the water.

Zarda tried to fly, but her wing hit a rock and got injured. She cried out, "Ouch! My wing hurts!"

The rabbit was also exhausted and couldn't swim very well.

Page 6

A Friend from the River

While they were struggling to stay afloat, a friendly dolphin appeared! He quickly swam over, helped them out of the water, and brought them safely to the shore.

The rabbit said to Zarda, "I'm so sorry, Zarda. I'm hurt and tired, and I can't go any further. I have to go back home."

Zarda felt sad, but she said, "Thank you for helping me." Then, she continued her journey alone.

Page 7

Night in the Forest

Zarda kept walking and walking until night began to fall. The forest grew dark, and she started feeling scared of wild animals.

She looked around until she found a small hollow inside a tree trunk. She thought, "I'll stay here until morning comes."

Zarda stepped inside the hollow and fell asleep, tired and frightened.

Page 8

The Squirrel

When the sun rose, Zarda woke up. Suddenly, she saw a little squirrel staring at her in surprise. He asked, "Who are you? And what are you doing in my home?"

Zarda apologized and told him her entire story since she flew away from her nest.

The squirrel listened attentively and said, "Don't worry, maybe I can help you!" He thought for a second and added, "My friend the owl knows every corner of this forest. I'll go and ask her!"

Page 9

The Owl Knows the Way

The squirrel quickly ran off and told the owl about Zarda's story.

The owl pondered for a moment and said, "A yellow flag near a bird's nest? I think I know that spot!"

Then she said, "Follow me, I'll take you there." Zarda was overjoyed and set off with the squirrel and the owl.

Page 10

Returning Home

The owl flew above the treetops while the squirrel and Zarda followed her until they reached a familiar place.

Suddenly, Zarda saw something bright and yellow glowing among the trees. She shouted with joy, "The yellow flag! That's it! We found my home!"

Zarda hurried toward her nest.

Page 11

Mother's Warm Hug

As soon as Zarda's mother saw her daughter, she flew straight to her and pulled her into a tight hug. The mother was crying happy tears.

She said, "Zarda! Where have you been? I was so worried about you!"

Zarda wept too and said, "I'm so sorry, Mommy. I was wrong to fly away from the nest and disobey you. I promise I will never go far alone again, and I will always listen to your advice."

The mother smiled, held her even closer, and said, "The most important thing is that you're back home safe and sound, my little one."

Page 12

A Beautiful Lesson 🌸

From that day on, Zarda learned an important lesson: she understood that listening to her parents' advice keeps her safe from danger, and that asking kind friends for help can save us when we're in trouble.

Zarda lived happily with her mother in their cozy little nest... and whenever she wanted to fly far away, she would always say, "I'll ask my Mommy first!" 🐦💗
