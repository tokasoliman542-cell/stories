🌸 Solomon's Ring

Page 1

The Peaceful Riverbank

Once upon a time, a kind girl named Layla lived with her mother on the bank of a peaceful river. Its water sparkled like a mirror made of silver.

Page 2

The Cozy Home

Their house was small but warm, filled with love, laughter, and colorful flowers blooming all around it.

Page 3

Mother's Advice

One day, Layla's mother was feeling unwell. Before leaving for work, she said:

"Be careful, Layla, and don't wander far from the river."

Page 4

The Promise

Layla nodded and promised her mother, waving with a sweet smile as she watched her walk down the long path.

Page 5

Layla's Idea

Feeling a bit bored, Layla looked at the river and thought:

"I'll catch a big fish for my mother so she can have a delicious meal when she returns."

Page 6

A Gentle Song

Layla sat quietly near the water, cast her fishing rod, and began singing a sweet song for the birds and butterflies around her.

Page 7

A Sudden Tug!

Suddenly... the fishing line tugged violently! Layla almost lost her balance from the strong pull.

Page 8

The Rainbow Fish

Layla pulled with all her strength, and out came a magnificent, huge fish whose scales shivered with strange rainbow colors.

Page 9

The Mysterious Ring

Layla placed the fish on the grass. As she carefully opened it to clean it, she found a golden ring with extraordinary engravings inside!

Page 10

The Voice of the Ring

The moment Layla touched the ring, she heard a gentle voice say:

"I am the Ring of Wisdom. I only work with kind hearts."

Page 11

One Wish

Layla trembled for a second, but then she remembered her mother's warmth and goodness, and smiled. The ring said:

"I shall grant you one wish, so use it for good."

Page 12

A Pure Wish

Layla thought to herself: "Should I ask for gold? Or a grand palace?" Then she said aloud:

"I wish for my mother to be healthy and well, and for us to live happily together without needing anyone."

Page 13

The Radiant Light

The ring glowed with a dazzling light that filled the entire space. Then, the voice faded away, and peace returned to the riverbank.

Page 14

Healing and Happiness

Layla ran back home to find her mother standing in great health, and the house filled with delicious food and tranquility.

Page 15

The Moral 🌸

From that day on, Layla learned that goodness always finds its way back to those who share it, and they lived together in everlasting happiness and peace.

🌸 The End 🌸
