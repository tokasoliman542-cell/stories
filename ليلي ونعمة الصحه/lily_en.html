👧 Lily and the Gift of Health

Page 1

A New Morning

Every morning, the sun rose gently, and little birds sang in the trees. Children got ready for school, carrying their colorful backpacks.

Among them was Lily, a little girl with a lovely smile. But Lily did not like going to school. She wished she could stay home and play all day!

Page 2

A Strange Wish

Every night before going to sleep, Lily would whisper to herself:

“I wish I could get a little sick tomorrow, so I could stay home instead of going to school!”

Lily did not understand that being sick could bring pain and tiredness, or that good health was a precious gift to be thankful for every day.

Page 3

Sudden Pain

One chilly morning, Lily woke up and began getting ready for school as usual.

As she put on her shoes, she suddenly felt a sharp pain in her tummy.

She placed her little hand on her tummy and whispered, “Oh! What is this pain?”

She tried to stand up, but the pain grew worse, and she could not finish getting ready.

Page 4

Mommy, Help Me!

Lily called out in a trembling voice:

“Mommy! Please come quickly! My tummy hurts so much!”

Her mother hurried into the room, sat beside her, and held her gently.

“I’m here, sweetheart,” Mom said kindly. “Don’t be afraid. We’ll get you some help.” She wiped Lily’s tears and helped her.

Page 5

To the Hospital

Mom took Lily to the hospital to find out what was causing the pain.

On the way, she held her daughter’s hand, comforted her, and prayed for her recovery.

“We’ll take good care of you, Lily,” she said. “The doctors will examine you and help you feel better.”

Lily felt a little calmer because she was not alone.

Page 6

The Kind Doctor

At the hospital, the doctor welcomed Lily with a kind smile.

He asked her about her pain and carefully examined her with his stethoscope while her mother held her hand.

“Well done, Lily! You’ve been very brave,” the doctor said. “We’ll help you feel better.”

Lily felt more comfortable knowing that people were taking care of her.

Page 7

Days in the Hospital

After the examination, the doctor told Mom that Lily needed to stay in the hospital for a few days to receive treatment.

Lily was not happy. She missed her bedroom and her toys.

“I want to go home, Mommy,” she said sadly.

Mom hugged her and replied, “I know, sweetheart. We’ll go home when the doctor says it’s safe.”

Page 8

A Changed Wish

The days passed slowly. Lily took her medicine and followed the doctor’s instructions.

One day, she remembered her old wish and thought sadly:

“I wished I would get sick so I could miss school, but I never knew how tiring being sick could be!”

Then she told herself, “I hope I get better soon. I’ll thank God for my health.”

Page 9

My Family Beside Me

Lily was not alone in the hospital.

Her mother sat beside her, and her father visited her and told her wonderful stories.

Sometimes they laughed together, and sometimes Lily rested in her mother’s arms.

Lily felt loved and safe. She learned that having family beside us can make difficult days easier.

Page 10

Friends Visiting

One lovely day, Lily’s school friends came to visit her.

They brought colorful cards, beautiful flowers, and a little gift.

Lily was delighted. “I’m so happy you came to see me! I’ve missed you,” she said.

Her friends smiled and replied, “We hope you get well soon and come back to play with us!”

Page 11

Thank God, I'm Getting Better!

By God’s grace, with treatment and her family’s care, Lily began to feel better day by day.

Her energy gradually returned, and she smiled more often.

“You’re getting better, Lily,” the doctor said. “We’ll let you know when it’s time to go home.”

Lily was very happy. “Thank God for the gift of health! I’ll take care of myself.”

Page 12

Lily's Happy Return 🌸

At last, the day came when Lily returned home!

After she had recovered, she woke up on her first day back at school, got dressed, and picked up her colorful backpack with a lovely smile.

She sat with her mother and told her what had been bothering her about school, and they agreed to work on those problems together.

Then Lily headed out happily, saying: "Thank God for the gift of health and wellness!" 👧💗
